# Career Progression Policy

This document outlines the career progression policy for engineering roles, detailing the levels, responsibilities, and criteria for promotion within the organization.

## Engineering Levels and Responsibilities

| Level | Designation                   | Responsibilities                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| E1    | **Intern**                    | Learns Verilog/SystemVerilog, RTL concepts, simulation, basic testbenches, assertions, scripting, Git, and the front-end development flow under close supervision.                                                                                               |
| E2    | **Trainee Engineer**          | Performs guided RTL coding, test development, simulation, debugging, scripting, and basic verification tasks.                                                                                                                                                    |
| E3    | **Junior Engineer**           | Independently handles small RTL blocks, verification components, tests, assertions, and basic functional debugging. Supervises interns in day-to-day technical work and provides guidance, feedback, and support.                                                |
| E4    | **Assistant Engineer**        | Owns moderately complex features or small blocks, guides interns and trainees on assigned work, and participates in code reviews, verification planning, debugging, and issue resolution.                                                                        |
| E5    | **Associate Engineer**        | Owns complete RTL blocks or verification environments, guides junior and assistant engineers on feature delivery, and is responsible for implementation, testing, debugging, and documentation.                                                                  |
| E6    | **Engineer**                  | Independently delivers complex RTL/DV work, supervises and develops junior, assistant, and associate engineers on assigned features, and handles functional debug, coverage, assertions, lint/CDC issues, integration, and block-level quality.                  |
| E7    | **Senior Associate Engineer** | Owns complex blocks or features, mentors engineers through the associate level, reviews their work, and contributes to verification methodology and implementation strategy.                                                                                     |
| E8    | **Senior Engineer**           | Acts as technical owner for major IP blocks or significant DV domains, guides senior associate and lower-level engineers, and drives block microarchitecture, verification planning, coverage closure, debug, quality, and delivery.                             |
| E9    | **Lead Engineer**             | Leads an RTL or DV technical area, guides senior engineers and technical owners, and owns subsystem execution, technical planning, integration, architecture discussions, major debug issues, reviews, and engineering methodology.                              |
| E10   | **Principal Engineer**        | Provides technical authority across multiple IPs or subsystems, guides leads and senior engineers, defines design and verification strategy, establishes engineering standards, resolves complex technical issues, and directs multiple teams.                   |
| E11   | **Principal Architect**       | Owns high-level architecture across major subsystems, guides principal engineers and subsystem leads, makes system-level RTL/microarchitecture decisions, defines interfaces and trade-offs, drives performance/PPA considerations, and aligns RTL and DV teams. |
| E12   | **Distinguished Engineer**    | Provides organization-wide technical leadership, guides principal engineers and architects, defines long-term front-end technology direction, advances methodologies and architectural practices, and leads critical technical initiatives.                      |

## Career Progression Policy

### 1) Probation Status

- E1 and E2 are probationary levels.
- Employees at E1 and E2 are not permanent employees.

### 2) E1 to E2 Progression

- Initial probation at E1 is 3 months.
- At the end of month 3:
  - If E2 responsibilities are met, promote to E2.
  - If E2 responsibilities are not met, extend probation by 1 month.
- At the end of the extension month:
  - If E2 responsibilities are met, promote to E2.
  - If E2 responsibilities are not met, terminate employment.

### 3) E2 to E3 Progression

- The same timeline and decision logic used for E1 to E2 applies to E2 to E3.
- Assessment is based on meeting E3 responsibilities.

### 4) Progression From E3 Onward

- From E3 onward, performance is reviewed annually.
- Promotion is based on the level that best matches demonstrated responsibilities, scope, and impact.

### 5) Accelerated Promotion

- Accelerated progression is allowed when criteria are clearly met.
- An employee at E3 may be promoted directly to E7 if performance and scope consistently meet E7 expectations.

## Role Evaluation Principles

Promotion and role review decisions should consider:

- Sustained delivery at the current level.
- Clear evidence of next-level scope and ownership.
- Quality of technical, operational, or people leadership outcomes.
- Growth in independence, complexity handled, and organizational influence.
- Mentorship, collaboration, documentation quality, and contribution to engineering practices.
- The difference between temporary project assignments and consistent role-level behavior.

This framework defines expectations, not a fixed career path. Growth may be technical, people-focused, or a combination of both.

# Engineering Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Leave (AL)

- **Eligibility:** Available to confirmed employees only. Interns and Trainee Engineers during probation follow their respective leave policies.
- **Allowance:** 20 days per fiscal year.
- **Usage:** Intended for personal matters and planned time off. **Unapproved absences may incur disciplinary action.**
- **Notice:** Submit planned leave requests at least 2 weeks in advance. **Leave requires explicit approval before absence.** Medical emergencies are an exception and fall under the Sick Leave policy. Other unplanned absences may be subject to review and approval based on the circumstances.
- **Duration:** Maximum 5 consecutive business days per request.
- **Medical Absence:** AL cannot be used to extend Sick Leave. If illness occurs during approved AL, the employee may request reclassification under the Sick Leave policy.
- **Carryover and Encashment:** Unused AL expires at fiscal year-end without carryover or payout, except where required by employment terms.

## 2. Sick Leave (SL)

- **Allowance:** 14 days per fiscal year. Additional medically necessary leave may be granted based on the recommendation of a healthcare professional.
- **Extended Absence:** Continuous sick leave exceeding 10 working days is unpaid from the 11th working day, regardless of unused annual sick-leave allowance. However, exceptions may be considered based on medical recommendations.
- **Notification:** **Notify the management as soon as reasonably practicable via EMS portal**, preferably before 11:00 AM on the day of absence.
- **Incapacity:** If you cannot notify the management yourself, notify them as soon as reasonable, directly or through an appropriate contact.
- **Medical Documentation:** A doctor's report or other appropriate medical evidence may be required for more than 2 working days of absence within a rolling 30-day period.
- **Confidentiality:** **Submit medical documents privately to the designated authority, not in public team channels.**

## 3. Handover, On-Call, and Deployment

- **Deployment Restrictions:** AL may be restricted for designated critical-role engineers during announced major production deployments or high-severity incidents. Exceptions may be considered for urgent circumstances.
- **Handover:** Before planned leave, document active tasks, open issues/PRs, pending reviews, and relevant responsibilities, and arrange handover to a colleague.
- **Meeting Overlap:** If leave overlaps with an assigned client meeting, arrange a qualified replacement and notify your team leader at least 48 hours in advance where practicable.
- **Emergency Absence:** Handover and notification requirements shall be applied reasonably when illness or emergencies prevent advance arrangements.
